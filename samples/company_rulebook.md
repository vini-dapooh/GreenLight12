# Dancing Fish – Packaging Label Standards (internal, v3)

Applies to every carton released in North America. Where this document is stricter than regulation, this document wins.

1. Every on-pack claim must be supported by the ingredient list and the nutrition panel. For example "No Added Sugar" cannot appear if any sweetener ingredient is present.
2. The ingredient list on the artwork must match the approved content sheet word for word, including percentages.
3. Marketing copy, storage instructions, allergen statement and dietary statements must match the approved content sheet exactly.
4. The net weight must match the content sheet and must be shown on the principal display panel in both ounces and grams.
5. Vegan or vegetarian claims and the Certified Vegan mark may only be used when no animal-derived ingredient (honey, milk, egg, gelatin...) is present.
6. Nutrition values must be internally consistent: %DV, calories and serving size arithmetic must reconcile.
7. Only one nutrition declaration format per pack: US Nutrition Facts. No kJ / "Salt" / "Saturates" strips, and no leftover UK panels.
8. The print file must not contain hidden or non-printing text objects.
9. The barcode must decode and must equal the UPC on the content sheet.
10. Every certification mark listed on the content sheet (USDA Organic, Certified Vegan, How2Recycle) must appear on the pack.
11. Allergens must be listed in a "Contains:" statement; "May contain" is reserved for genuine cross-contact risks.
12. The brand logo must keep clear space equal to the height of the letter D on all sides.
13. The front panel must show the product photography at no less than 60% of panel width.
